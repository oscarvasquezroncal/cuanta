# Historias de usuario — cuanta

**Budget:** the backlog, READ at the start of every mandate (open entries only) and WRITTEN at
the end. **Max 15 open entries** (`pendiente` + `en progreso`); the docs-updater consolidates
over it. Three lines of substance per entry — never a transcript. What does NOT belong here:
narrative (`docs/CHANGELOG_INTERNAL.md`), gaps (`docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`).

**Entry shape (parsed — keep the field names):**

```
## HU-NNN — <short title>
- Estado: pendiente | en progreso | hecha | descartada   (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Contexto: <one line: the symptom or the want>
- Depende de: HU-NNN | —
- Cierre: <audit/changelog link, when hecha>
```
